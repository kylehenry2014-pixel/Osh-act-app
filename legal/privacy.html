OHS Act & Regulations

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the OHS Act & Regulations app collects, how it is used, and how to reach us with questions. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than dense legal text.

Summary

You do not need to create a personal account to use this app. The app signs you in anonymously in the background so we can enforce daily usage limits on certain features and remember your subscription status - this does not identify you personally. Bookmarks, notes, saved certificates, and photos you add stay on your own device. The app shows ads (unless you have an active "Remove Ads" subscription), uses an AI assistant for two features, and lets you save documents to your device.

Anonymous sign-in and usage limits

When you open the app, it automatically signs you in anonymously using Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier for your device - it is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal account. We use this identifier only to:

  • Enforce daily usage limits on the Safety Chat Bot, SDS Finder, and document downloads (free users get a limited number of uses per day; Pro subscribers get a higher limit)
  • Remember whether you have an active "Remove Ads" subscription

This usage-count and subscription-status information is stored on our servers (Google Firestore), tied only to the anonymous device identifier, not to you personally.

Safety Chat Bot and SDS Finder

When you use the Safety Chat Bot or SDS Finder, the text you type (and, for SDS Finder, any photo you take of a chemical label) is sent to our backend server and then to Anthropic, our AI provider, to generate a response. This content is used only to answer your question and is not used to train AI models or shared for any other purpose. Photos you submit to SDS Finder are processed to identify the product and are not permanently stored by us.

What the app stores only on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never sent to our servers: bookmarks, personal notes on Act/Regulation sections, saved Certificate Reminders (including any photos you add of physical certificates), and your language preference. If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted along with it.

Advertising

Unless you have an active "Remove Ads" subscription, the app shows ads via Google's AdMob service to support free access to its features. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, in line with Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device's ad settings, and you can remove ads entirely with a Pro subscription.

Subscriptions and purchases

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" subscription, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a valid purchase was made (so we can enable your Pro features) but do not receive or store your payment details - these are handled by Google Play, not by us.

What happens when you submit a bug report

The app includes an optional "Report a bug" form. If you choose to use it, the following is sent to Formspree, a third-party form processing service, which forwards it to us by email:

  • The description you typed into the form
  • The section of the app you were viewing at the time, if any
  • Basic technical information about your device or browser

This information is used only to understand and fix the issue you reported. We do not use it for marketing.

What the app does not do

  • No personal account, name, or email is required to use the app
  • No selling of your information to third parties
  • No sharing of your Chat Bot/SDS Finder content beyond what's needed to generate a response

Children's privacy

This app is a professional reference tool for occupational health and safety law and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date at the top of the page.
