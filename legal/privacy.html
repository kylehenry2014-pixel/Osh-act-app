OHS Act & Regulations

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what information the OHS Act & Regulations app collects, how it is used, and how to reach us with questions. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than dense legal text.

Summary

The app does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect, store, or share your personal information on any server. Bookmarks, notes, and checklist progress you create in the app stay on your own device. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you choose to type into the optional bug report form.

What the app stores on your device

If you use the Bookmarks, Notes, or Checklist features, the app saves that information locally on your phone using standard device storage. This never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is not sent to any server. If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted along with it.

What happens when you submit a bug report

The app includes an optional "Report a bug" form. If you choose to use it, the following is sent to Formspree, a third party form processing service, which forwards it to us by email:

  • The description you typed into the form
  • The section of the app you were viewing at the time, if any
  • Basic technical information about your device or browser

This information is used only to understand and fix the issue you reported. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not share it with anyone beyond what is needed to process the report.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for marketing purposes
  • No account or login is required to use the app

Children's privacy

This app is a professional reference tool for occupational health and safety law and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Future updates to this policy

Planned features such as optional paid subscriptions and user accounts are not yet part of the app. When they are introduced, this policy will be updated in advance to describe exactly what additional information is involved, and the update will be clearly dated so you can see what has changed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date at the top of the page.
