## Full Description (4,000 char limit, both stores)

The complete South African Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993, together with all 25 key regulation sets, in one searchable app.

WHY THIS APP
Every section of the OHS Act and its regulations, sourced directly from the Government Gazette and checked for the current, in-force version. No outdated text, no summaries standing in for the real law. If a regulation has been replaced or amended, this app reflects the version that actually applies today.

WHAT'S INCLUDED, FREE
- The full OHS Act 85 of 1993, all 50 sections
- All 25 regulation sets: General, Health, Mechanical, and Electrical
- Fast search across every section and regulation
- Quick-jump codes for direct lookup (e.g. "CR 8" for Construction Regulations, Regulation 8)
- Bookmarks and personal notes on any section, saved on your device
- Read-aloud text-to-speech for hands-free reference on site
- Afrikaans translations (in progress), with English always available as the authoritative text
- ISO 45001, ISO 9001, and ISO 14001 guidance, cross-linked to the relevant South African regulations
- Practical site checklists (working at heights, confined space entry, hazardous chemicals, asbestos work, and more), each linked to the regulation and ISO clause it satisfies

WHO IT'S FOR
Safety officers, HR and EHS managers, business owners, site supervisors, and anyone studying for a NOSA or SAMTRAC qualification who needs the actual legal text at hand, not a paraphrase.

BUILT FOR ACCURACY
This app exists because safety law shouldn't be guessed at. Every entry is checked against the current Government Gazette. Where a regulation has changed, the app is updated to match, not left stale.

No account required. Your bookmarks and notes stay on your device.
